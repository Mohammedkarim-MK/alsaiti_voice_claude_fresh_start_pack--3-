Know your goldfish

The varieties we specialise in

Every one of these is drawn to life below — scroll sideways and watch them swim.

Oranda

The classic hooded goldfish. A raspberry-like wen that grows for years.

Ranchu

No dorsal fin, deep arched back. Judged from above in Japan.

Ryukin

Dramatic shoulder hump and long flowing finnage. A show-stopper.

Black Moor

Velvet black with telescope eyes. Gentle, and forgiving for beginners.

Lionhead

Heavy wen, smooth back. The ancestor of the modern ranchu.

Pearlscale

Domed scales like tiny pearls on a golf-ball body.

Bubble Eye

Delicate fluid sacs. Needs a bare, gentle tank — for experienced keepers.

Shubunkin

Calico single-tail. Hardy, fast, and perfect for ponds.
